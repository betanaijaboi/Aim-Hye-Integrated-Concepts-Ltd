# Privacy Policy — Aim-Hye Integrated Concepts Ltd

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## What we collect

- **Account info:** name, email, phone, delivery address(es).
- **Authentication data:** password (bcrypt-hashed) or passkey/biometric
  credential (WebAuthn) — biometric data itself never leaves your device;
  we only store the cryptographic public key WebAuthn generates.
- **Order data:** items ordered, quantities, pricing, delivery address,
  order history.
- **Invoicing data:** used to generate order invoices/documentation.

## Why we collect it

To create your account, process and deliver orders, generate invoices,
and communicate order status via email.

## Who we share it with

- **Delivery/logistics partners** — your delivery address and order
  details, to fulfil the order.
- **Database provider** (Turso/libSQL) and email provider (via
  nodemailer) — infrastructure needed to run the service.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Your rights (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023)

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any
time. Order and invoice records may be retained for the period required
under Nigerian tax/accounting recordkeeping rules even after a deletion
request.

## Security

Passwords are bcrypt-hashed. Passkey/biometric login uses WebAuthn, which
never transmits your actual biometric data — only a device-generated
cryptographic signature.
